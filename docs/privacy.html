Privacy Policy for Roman Urdu Keyboard

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Roman Urdu Keyboard ("the app") is an Android keyboard by Paul Eggington ("I", "me"). It translates and types Roman Urdu mostly on your phone, with no account and no server run by me.

What stays on your phone

  • Everything you type. A keyboard can see what you type. The app doesn't send your typing, messages, passwords or contacts to me or to anyone else.
  • Translation and voice features. Translation, the Voice card (Urdu voice note to English text, English speech to an Urdu audio file) and the spelling suggestions all run on the on-device AI model. Your text and audio don't leave your phone for these.
  • Learned words, settings and clipboard history. These are stored only on your device. You can clear them in the app, or by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

What is sent over the internet

These are the only cases. All of them use HTTPS.

  1. GIF and sticker search. If you search for a GIF or sticker, your search words are sent to GIPHY (api.giphy.com) to get results. GIPHY's own privacy policy applies to that data.
  2. Voice typing with the keyboard's microphone button. This uses Android's built-in speech recognition. Depending on your device and settings, your audio may be processed by Google's speech service. Google's privacy policy applies.
  3. Downloading the AI model. On first setup the app downloads the model file from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). Hugging Face receives the usual connection details, such as your IP address. If you choose to enter a Hugging Face access token, it is sent to Hugging Face only to authorize the download, and it stays on your device.

Permissions

  • Microphone: used only when you press a voice button.
  • Internet: used only for the three cases above.
  • Notifications or storage access, if requested: used only for the feature you're using, such as saving or sharing an audio file you created.

What I don't do

  • I don't have accounts, and I don't collect your name, email, location or device identifiers.
  • I don't run ads or analytics, and I don't use tracking SDKs.
  • I don't sell or share your data with anyone else, apart from the third-party requests listed above, which you trigger yourself.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update the date above and publish the new version at this address.
