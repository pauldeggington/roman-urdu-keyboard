Texting in Roman Urdu usually means spelling every word out by hand and hoping it reads right, or giving up and writing English.

Roman Urdu Keyboard does the translating for you, in the tone you pick, and everything stays on your phone.

with the translator inside

You never switch apps to translate. Type, tap one key, and send.

Say it in the right tone

Pick casual, formal, poetic, funny or respectful from the toolbar, or leave it on Auto and the keyboard chooses from what you wrote. It also handles male and female wording: thak gaya or thak gayi.

badha→barha بڑھا
parna→parhna پڑھنا
pool→phool پھول

Spelling that follows the Urdu letters

Roman Urdu has no standard spelling, so the keyboard follows the Urdu letters instead of Hindi habits. Add your own spellings in the glossary (bada → barha) and it uses them every time.

Emoji, GIFs, stickers, clipboard

Emoji with search and skin tones, text emoticons in groups, GIFs and stickers from GIPHY, and a clipboard history like Gboard's. Tap to paste, long-press to pin.

Types like Gboard

The same layout and height, glide typing in English, word suggestions, next-word hints, and autocorrect you can undo with one Backspace. Light, dark, or your wallpaper colours.

Voice, both ways

Share an Urdu voice note from WhatsApp to the app and read it in English. Or speak English and get an Urdu voice message to send. It runs on the phone and needs the Gemma 4 model.

Works offline

The small Gemma 3 translator is inside the app, so it works the first time you open it. For better translations and voice, download Gemma 4 once (2.6 GB). Neither needs a connection afterwards.

Stays on your phone

What you type never leaves the phone. There is no account, no analytics and no ads. Words it learns stay on the device, and it never learns from password fields.

No ads, no account

There is nothing to sign up for and nothing is tracked. Get it from Google Play, or download the APK from GitHub and install it yourself.

Android 12 or newer on a 64-bit phone, with a few gigabytes free. I built and tested it on a Pixel 8a, and phones with more memory translate faster.
